1RM Estimator — Privacy Policy — Last updated: 2026-10-25

Summary: Your entered workout values and estimation history stay on your device. Optional usage analytics are off by default and require your explicit choice. Crash reporting and advertising are described separately below.

1. Data stored on your device

Predict stores the following data on your device. Workout values and history remain local; selected preferences may also be sent only if you allow the optional analytics described below:

  • Last entered weight, reps, and RPE values (to pre-fill the form)
  • Unit preference (kg / lbs) and rounding preference
  • Estimation history (up to 50 entries)

Entered weight, reps, RPE and estimation history are not sent through usage analytics. If you allow analytics, preference values may be sent as described below.

Optional usage analytics

On first launch (or the first launch after upgrading from a version without a recorded decision), you can choose Allow or No thanks. Your decision is remembered; refusing does not limit app features or trigger repeated requests. You can enable or disable usage analytics anytime in Settings. This choice is separate from advertising consent.

Only after Allow, Predict sends the usage event names app_opened, estimate_calculated, history_opened, settings_opened, result_shared, unit_changed, rounding_changed and theme_changed to Mixpanel EU (api-eu.mixpanel.com), with unit, rounding and theme preferences, a random installation ID, app version, Android platform and event time. The random ID is pseudonymous, not anonymous. No weight, reps, RPE, calculated estimates or estimation history are included.

Disabling analytics blocks new requests, cancels pending work and removes the local analytics ID. Data already transmitted cannot be retracted by the switch. Enabling again uses a new random ID. Legal basis: consent. Processor: Mixpanel.

2. Crash reporting

Predict uses Sentry for anonymous crash and error reporting.

  • No personal data is included in crash reports
  • Reports contain: stack trace, Android version, device model, app version
  • Data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany)
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (app stability)
  • Data processor: Sentry — sentry.io/privacy

3. Advertising

Predict displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads.

  • A consent dialog is shown before personalised ads are displayed (GDPR)
  • You can withdraw or change your consent at any time in the app settings
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings
  • AdMob privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. No account required

Predict does not require registration or login. No email address or name is required. Optional analytics uses the pseudonymous installation ID described above.

5. Children

Predict is intended for users aged 16 and older. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. You can delete locally stored app data via Android Settings → Apps → Predict → Clear Data.

For requests concerning data held by Sentry or optional Mixpanel analytics, contact us using the link below.
